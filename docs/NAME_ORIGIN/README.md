## Names in Different Languages

**Chinese (中文):**  
予智機管理器 (yǔ zhì jī guǎn lǐ qì)

**Japanese (日本語):**  
YU AI マネージャー

**English:**  
YU AI Manager

---

## Why Different Names?

The Chinese name uses **智機** (zhì jī, "intelligent machine") as a neologism for AI.

The Japanese and English names use **"AI"** (Artificial Intelligence) as the standard term.

**Why?**

Because:
- "AI" is the established term in Japanese and English
- 智機 is a Chinese neologism not commonly used in Japanese/English
- Each language uses its natural terminology

## The Eight Meanings of YU (予)

All rooted in Chinese characters:

1. **I (余)** - My tool
2. **Beforehand (豫)** - Predetermined  
3. **With (與)** - Together with AI
4. **In (於)** - In the AI era
5. **Surplus (餘)** - Creative surplus (1+1=3)
6. **Friend (友)** - AI as friend
7. **Excellence (優)** - Beautiful and excellent
8. **Play (遊)** - Playfulness in work

Eight meanings in one sound.  
Like the Eight Virtues (八德).  
Like the Eight Trigrams (八卦).

By coincidence, sounds like English "You".  
Not sought, yet fitting. How wonderful.
