yu_ai_manager does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All processing occurs locally on your device.
§ 01

Data collection

yu_ai_manager does not collect any personal information. The application operates entirely on your local machine. No data is sent to external servers by the application itself.

no collection no analytics no crash reports
§ 02

Local storage

The application stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Image library metadata (file paths, tags, ratings) in a local SQLite database
  • Application configuration and preferences
  • AI analysis results generated on your device
  • Prompt library entries you create

This data never leaves your device and is under your full control. You may delete it at any time by removing the application data directory.

§ 03

Network activity

yu_ai_manager may make network connections in the following cases, all initiated by you:

  • Connections to AI inference servers you configure (local network only by default)
  • Connections to external APIs (Stable Diffusion WebUI, NovelAI, etc.) that you explicitly configure
  • mDNS / Bonjour discovery on your local network for peer inference nodes

No telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting data is transmitted. The application does not “phone home.”

§ 04

Third‑party services

If you configure the application to connect to third‑party services (e.g., NovelAI, OpenAI‑compatible APIs), those services operate under their own privacy policies. yu_ai_manager does not control or mediate that data flow.

§ 05

Microsoft Store

Purchase and distribution through the Microsoft Store is subject to Microsoft's privacy policy. The Store may collect purchase information as per their standard terms. This is outside the scope of yu_ai_manager itself.

§ 06

Open source

The complete source code for yu_ai_manager is available at github.com/eauesque/yu_ai_manager under the MIT License. You are free to audit the code to verify these claims.

In practice: git clone the repository and ask any AI assistant to review it. Network activity, data collection, and telemetry — or the absence thereof — are verifiable in minutes. We encourage this.

§ 07

Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be directed via the GitHub repository linked above.
